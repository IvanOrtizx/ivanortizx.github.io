Chapter 3 · The experiment

It all started with one question: what if we told our region's story with AI?

In October 2026 we launched @ia_huila to test an idea: exaggerated, surreal and funny AI videos about Huila, our region in Colombia. Our city in the year 2099, ancient statues coming to life, witches flying over town. It worked faster than we ever imagined.
