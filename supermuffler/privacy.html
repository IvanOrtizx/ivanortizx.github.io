Privacy policy

What we collect

When you use the contact form on this website we receive the information you type: your name, phone number and, if you add them, your email, vehicle, what you need and your message. We also note the language you used, how you heard about us, the page you came from and whether you agreed to be contacted or to receive offers.

How we use it

  • To call, text or email you about your request.
  • To send you specials and offers, only if you checked that box. You can ask us to stop at any time.
  • To understand which ads and social media bring customers to the shop.

Who can see it

Your information is sent by email to Super Muffler Inc. through Web3Forms, the service that delivers our website forms. We do not sell or rent your information.

Other services on this page

The map is provided by Google Maps, and videos and links may come from TikTok, Instagram and Facebook. Those services have their own privacy policies and may set their own cookies when you use them. This website does not use advertising cookies of its own.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete the information you sent us, or to stop receiving texts or offers, call us at (805) 922-9684 or ask at the counter at 926 W Main St, Santa Maria, CA 93458. If you reply STOP to a text from us, we will stop texting you.
